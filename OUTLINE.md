# Adopted writing architecture

**Emulate:** Nature-family *methods* article (gap → method → fair ablation → calibration/utility as **evaluation** → reproducibility → boundary).

**Spine (Chinese, current):** `manuscript_zh_v2.md` → `manuscript_zh_v3.docx` (43 pages, 17 tables, 16 figures, 9 numbered equations, 24 references, live TOC). HTML and PDF companions are `manuscript_zh_v3.html` and `manuscript_zh_v3.pdf`, regenerated by `export_chinese_html.py` and the Word export step.

**English companion:** `manuscript.md` (earlier draft; kept for the public repo snapshot).

**Innovation claims (bounded):** monotone bounded quality routing; masked partial-label multitask against the released-code independent loops; quality-conditioned backbone routing (E5); the logit-prevalence offset diagnosis and its zero-parameter correction.

**Evaluation framework (not novelty):** temperature scaling, ECE, Brier, per-disease DCA, frozen validation thresholds.

**Non-claims:** synthetic AUROC as clinical evidence; beating 0.833 T2DM; ODIR-D as UKB T2DM; `diabetes_related` as a clinical head; calibration/DCA as methodological novelty.

## Draft risks

1. Two macro deltas have a lower CI bound at or near zero; they are written as boundary evidence.
2. Published "multitask" vs released independent-head loops.
3. Endpoint drift (`diabetes_related` / ocular ≠ UKB ICD).
4. Monotone router stays near (0, 0.5, 1), so the gain is traced to the training trajectory.
5. Threshold transfer is confounded with small-sample quantile jitter at n ≈ 600.
